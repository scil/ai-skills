Rows are ordered by **authority tier**, and the tier is the first part of `kind`:

| Tier | kind prefix | What it is | What it may do in this skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `T1-vendor` | The harness vendor's own documentation for its instruction file (Anthropic for Claude Code, OpenAI for Codex), plus the AGENTS.md format's own site | **Decides.** A T1 statement enters `rules.md` or `harness/*.md` on its own. Where Anthropic and OpenAI differ, both are recorded and the file follows the harness it is read by. |
| 2 | `T2-research` | Peer-reviewed or arXiv empirical studies | **Corroborates or qualifies.** A finding enters only when two independent studies agree, or when it explains a T1 rule. A lone study is recorded as "one study finds" and shapes no rule by itself. |
| 3 | `T3-exemplar` | Real instruction files in popular repositories | **Illustrates.** Shows a pattern in use; never the source of a rule. |
| 4 | `T4-report` | Vendor blogs, engineering posts, secondary coverage, guides | **Points.** Leads to a T1 or T2 source, or records a claim as "reported". An engineering post from a harness vendor about its own practice (OpenAI's Harness engineering) is T4 with weight: first-hand, but one team's account. |
