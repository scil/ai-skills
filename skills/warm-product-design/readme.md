# warm-product-design — origin

Founded in 2026-08 while designing the language of a gratitude product centred on a written thank-you card, named after a porch. That product's brand spec, token file and voice rules are its own facts of record now; the method here is what generalised.

The markers that made the method apply there, for recognising the next case: an emotional core (trust, care, gratitude, belonging, mutual help), users already connected offline, a spatial or sensory name (porch, table, hearth, lantern, letter), explicit anti-marketplace positioning, and low frequency with high meaning. A productivity tool, dashboard, e-commerce platform or utility is the wrong fit.
